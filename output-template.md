## Rendering rules

- **No emojis.** Audit reports go to people deciding what to ship; tone is utilitarian.
- **No fluff.** "Excellent work!" / "Looking great!" — cut. State the score, list the gaps.
- **Cite line numbers.** Every finding has `<file>:<line>`. Range form `:10-25` is fine.
- **Verbatim evidence.** Code/config blocks must be copy-pasted from the file, not paraphrased.
- **Fix sketch ≤2 lines.** This is a report, not a PR.
- **Phase sizing must be honest.** "1–2 days" of one engineer's time. If a phase is 2 weeks, say so.
- **Score guide footer mandatory.** Without it, scorecards are uninterpretable.
